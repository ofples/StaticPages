Chandas 2.4.0

Permission demonstrations for com.yukti.chandas. These are recordings of the Android release app on a dedicated Android 16 test emulator. The first recording includes the actual device audio. The test device contains only synthetic timer data.

Foreground service: media playback

A timer is configured and started by the user. A halfway bell plays once. The app is backgrounded, and its ongoing countdown is shown in the notification drawer. A configured alarm wakes the locked device and displays the alarm screen. Later, a second alarm is dismissed with the notification's Stop alarm action.

Approximate points in the recording: 0:20 start; 0:39 halfway cue; 0:55 lock-screen alarm; 1:15 ordinary cue at the end of the non-alarm step; 2:00 alarm dismissal. The ordinary Android alarm sound used as a cue plays once and stops; it does not become a persistent alarm.

Open the media-playback video directly

The media-playback foreground service keeps scheduled audible cues and alarms alive while the app is backgrounded or locked. Delaying them would miss the user-selected time. Ordinary cues end automatically; alarms can be dismissed.

Foreground service: live timer status

The user enables the floating timer in Appearance settings, starts a saved timer, and leaves the app. A small floating countdown remains visible over the home screen. The ongoing notification shows timer status and a Stop timer action. Stopping the timer removes the floating timer and ends the foreground service.

Open the live timer-status video directly

The special-use foreground service maintains visible status for active, user-started timers. It is used for the optional floating timer and, where Android permits it, the promoted live countdown. Display-over-other-apps permission is granted separately by the user. This emulator presents the standard ongoing notification rather than a status-bar chip.
